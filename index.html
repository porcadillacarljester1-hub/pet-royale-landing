Pet Royale.

App for Android phones

Book your pet’s clinic visit from your phone.

The Pet Royale app is for pet owners. Add your pets, request an appointment and follow your visits, all in one place.

Download the app (APK)

Version 1.0.3 · For Android phones only. Please read the install steps below before you open the file.

What you can do in the app

  • Add your pets

    Keep each pet’s details in your account.

  • Book an appointment

    Choose your pet, the service, a date and a time.

  • Follow your visits

    See which requests are waiting, confirmed or completed.

  • Get notified

    The app tells you when the clinic confirms or completes a visit.

  • See Pet Essentials

    Check the products the clinic has, with prices and stock. You buy them at the clinic.

  • Pay at the clinic

    Payment is in cash when you visit. There is no online payment.

How to install

  1. Download the fileTap the button above on your Android phone. If your browser asks, choose to keep the file.
  2. Open itTap the download notification, or find the file in your Downloads folder.
  3. Allow the install if askedAndroid may say the browser isn’t allowed to install apps. Turn on “Allow from this source”, then go back and tap Install. The wording differs between phones.
  4. Create your accountOpen Pet Royale, register with your details or sign in with Google, then add your pet.

The app is installed from this file and not from Google Play, so Android shows a warning before installing. Only install it from this page.
